    Model Comparison Guide¶

    Overview¶

    Sibyl supports local open-source LLMs running via Ollama. This guide helps you understand the characteristics of different models and choose the best one for your needs.

    Supported Models¶

    Llama 3.2 (Meta)¶

    Models: llama3.2:1b, llama3.2:3b

    Strengths: - Excellent general capabilities - Multiple size options for different hardware - Strong reasoning abilities - Well-optimized for local execution

    Best For: - General academic paper review - Balanced performance and quality - Users with moderate hardware

    Typical Characteristics: - Provides thorough, balanced analysis - Good at identifying both strengths and weaknesses - Reliable across different paper types

    Hardware: - 1B: 8GB RAM minimum - 3B: 16GB RAM recommended

    Mistral (Mistral AI)¶

    Model: mistral:7b

    Strengths: - Excellent reasoning and analytical capabilities - Strong attention to technical details - Good at identifying subtle issues - Fast inference relative to size

    Best For: - Technical papers requiring deep analysis - Complex methodologies - When detail-oriented feedback is crucial

    Typical Characteristics: - Thorough and methodical reviews - May be more critical than other models - Excellent at logical reasoning

    Hardware: 16GB RAM recommended, 32GB optimal

    Phi-3 (Microsoft)¶

    Models: phi3:mini (3.8B), phi3:medium (14B)

    Strengths: - Impressive performance for size - Efficient resource usage - Strong language understanding - Good general knowledge

    Best For: - Users with limited hardware - Quick reviews - General assessment needs

    Typical Characteristics: - Concise, focused reviews - Good balance of quality and speed - Efficient on consumer hardware

    Hardware: - Mini: 8GB RAM - Medium: 24GB RAM

    Gemma 2 (Google)¶

    Models: gemma2:2b, gemma2:9b

    Strengths: - Well-balanced capabilities - Good instruction following - Efficient architecture - Strong performance across tasks

    Best For: - General paper review - Multi-domain research - Balanced assessments

    Typical Characteristics: - Balanced, fair reviews - Good contextual understanding - Reliable outputs

    Hardware: - 2B: 8GB RAM - 9B: 16-24GB RAM

    Qwen 2.5 (Alibaba)¶

    Models: Various sizes from 0.5B to 72B

    Strengths: - Strong multilingual capabilities - Excellent reasoning abilities - Good at structured outputs - Multiple size options

    Best For: - Papers in multiple languages - Complex reasoning tasks - Structured analysis needs

    Typical Characteristics: - Detailed, structured reviews - Good at following complex instructions - Strong analytical capabilities

    Hardware: Varies by size (8GB to 64GB+ RAM)

    DeepSeek Coder (DeepSeek)¶

    Models: deepseek-coder:1.3b, deepseek-coder:6.7b, deepseek-coder:33b

    Strengths: - Exceptional understanding of code and technical content - Strong performance on technical and computer science papers - Good at analyzing algorithms and implementations - Excellent code snippet comprehension

    Best For: - Computer science papers - Papers with significant code components - Algorithm analysis - Technical documentation review

    Typical Characteristics: - Thorough technical analysis - Strong understanding of programming concepts - Good at identifying implementation issues - Detailed code-related feedback

    Hardware: - 1.3B: 8GB RAM - 6.7B: 16GB RAM - 33B: 64GB+ RAM

    Comparison Strategy¶

    When to Compare Multiple Models¶

    Sequential Comparison Beneficial: - High-stakes decisions (acceptance/rejection) - Controversial or novel work - When diverse perspectives are valuable - For comprehensive evaluation

    Single Model Sufficient: - Preliminary reviews of your own work - Quick quality checks - Informal assessments - When time/resources are limited

    Recommended Approaches¶

    Best Quality: Mistral or Llama 3.2:3b - Highest quality reviews - Best for critical decisions - Requires adequate hardware

    Balanced: Llama 3.2:1b or Phi-3 Mini - Good balance of quality and speed - Sufficient for most purposes - Works on modest hardware

    Fast/Efficient: Phi-3 Mini or Gemma 2:2b - Quick turnaround - Lower resource requirements - Good for initial screening

    Interpreting Results¶

    Score Tendencies¶

    Different models may have different scoring tendencies:

    • Mistral: Tends toward detailed, critical analysis
    • Llama: Balanced, uses full score range
    • Phi-3: Concise, focused feedback
    • Gemma: Balanced and fair
    • Qwen: Structured, detailed responses
    • DeepSeek: Technical focus, excellent for code/algorithms

    Focus Areas¶

    • Mistral: Heavy emphasis on methodology and rigor
    • Llama: Balanced across all criteria
    • Phi-3: Focuses on key issues
    • Gemma: Good at practical implications
    • Qwen: Strong on structured analysis
    • DeepSeek: Excels at technical and algorithmic content

    Best Practices¶

    Model Selection Tips¶

    1. Match hardware to model: Don't choose a model too large for your system
    2. Consider paper complexity: Use larger models for complex papers
    3. Experiment: Try different models to find what works for your domain
    4. Document preferences: Track which models work best for your field

    Optimizing for Your Needs¶

    For Speed: Phi-3 Mini, Gemma 2:2b, Llama 3.2:1b, DeepSeek 1.3b For Quality: Mistral, Llama 3.2:3b, Qwen (larger variants), DeepSeek 33b For Balance: Llama 3.2:3b, Gemma 2:9b, DeepSeek 6.7b For Limited Hardware: Phi-3 Mini, Llama 3.2:1b, DeepSeek 1.3b For Technical Papers: DeepSeek Coder (any size), Mistral

    Hardware Considerations¶

    GPU Acceleration¶

    Models run significantly faster with GPU acceleration: - NVIDIA GPUs: Best support via CUDA - Apple Silicon: Good support via Metal - AMD GPUs: Growing support via ROCm

    Memory Requirements¶

    Approximate RAM needed (for Q4 quantization): - 1-2B models: 4-8GB - 3-4B models: 8-12GB - 7-8B models: 12-20GB - 13-14B models: 24-32GB - 30B+ models: 64GB+

    Future Model Support¶

    Ollama regularly adds support for new models. Check ollama list for available models or visit ollama.ai/library for the complete list.

    Conclusion¶

    The best model depends on your specific needs, hardware constraints, and use case. Start with Llama 3.2 or Phi-3 Mini to understand their characteristics, then experiment with others based on your requirements.
